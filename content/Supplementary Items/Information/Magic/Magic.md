# Notable Terms
#### Parallel 
In reference to magic, this means that two manipulations exist exactly the sa[[Janus]]ings, they have no effect on each other, and ar[[Janus]] reference to magic, this means that said manipulation will destroy the other.

#### Absorption
In reference to magic, this means that said manipulation will collect the other, but will weaken itself in the process. For example, [[Magic#*Crystallokinesis*|Crystallokinesis]] crystals and stones will absorb [[Magic#*Electrikinesis*|Electrikinetic Magic]], but it can only take so much before the crystals begin to crack and shatter.

#### Enhancement
In reference to magic, this means that the two manipulations make each other stronger when combined by one or more users.

#### Paradoxicality
In reference to magic, this means that when these two manipulations are used simultaneously,. harm befalls both users.
