### *Log 1 - Connor Smith*

"The forest outside of Connor's porch was never still. Not completely."

`I can tell she is upset again. Reasonably so, and I can tell she yearns to drink again. Rei refuses to give in to that though, but I would still support her anyway. Lucielle has been quiet around me lately, but I know she is merely calculating. She has finally became curious about my scales, I'm just waiting on her inquisitions.. oh boy.`

Connor took his coat off as he walked out the door of their cabin. He draped it over the back of his chair on the porch before walking into the yard. He closed his eyes and enjoyed the feeling of the sun on his arms and face. Warmth helps ease his aches.

He sighed. Back to work.

He walked into the woods to collect some branches to patch the small holes between the logs of their cabin. He and Rei built it sometime in Spring earlier that year, and it was starting to get close to Winter, so he wanted to get ahead on insulating the house. Mud and daub should work, and he already planned for a fireplace, but has put off building it for some time. 

### *Log 2 - Leo*

I wish it didn't take Amanda Ivanov's death to make me realize how special Victoria was to me. I loved Amanda - for a short time - but if I really loved her, would I have done that? Even though she was conspiring against me, trying to use me? Her affection felt so real, and yet.. Victoria has given me something more. I don't know what exactly, but I've never been happier.

I wish she could read my mind. I look at her, and I am speechless. There is a pain in my chest from all of the words and actions I can never bring myself to say or do.. My thoughts aren't my own, I suppose.

I hear her coming down the hallway. The only three people that know I am still alive are Natz, Cat, and Victoria, (gratefully.) Natz is always stomping around brutishly, and I can never hear Cat when she comes. Victoria though, she skips.. or dashes.. hums sometimes. She is tapping her cute shoes on the metal grated floor, humming some song I have begun to recognize, only from her. I smile..

"Leeoo!!" She opened the door. Gently, light spills on to the floor of my room, reserve the figure of my Victoria.. Her pigtails were so curled that light shone through and I swear I could see bokeh illuminating from between those strands. Her beautiful smile, accompanied by, "I got a new set of cards! All 52 cards! I can finally teach you some games!!"

I grin, and I cry, and I forget. Then remember. These few blissful moments I have with her are fleeting, and soon gone. Then I wait and wait. And wait. If I didn't know that Cat fed me twice daily, I would think it was years before I got to see Vi again, but she has visited me every day, keeping me company since I killed Amanda. For this... I would kill her again.
