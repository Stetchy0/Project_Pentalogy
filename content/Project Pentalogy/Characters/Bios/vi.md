Vi is [[Leo]]'s abuser (See [[Situationship]]). She renames him [[Leonidas/Lee]] when she forces him into a relationship, basically through Stockholm syndrome. She was born in [[Farther Earth|farther earth]]. She is [[io]]'s twin. She was introduced in [[Part 0.4 - Prestige]].

She is the first successful [[Synthetic Nymph]] human experiment, and had gained extended life and permanent youth because of it. She was X's prized possession and major breakthrough in his [[Pursuit of Power]].
