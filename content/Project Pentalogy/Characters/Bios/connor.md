Connor Smith, originally born Carter Smith in [[Farther Earth|farther earth]], introduced in [[Part I - Experience]], is one of many of the [[Black Crusaders]]'s experiments, and arguably one of their most successful despite being one of the main factors leading to the organization's downfall. He is also one of the thousands of [[Kidnapped]], but one of the few that survive the crusaders. Connor's synthetic nymph is a snake, whom he overpowers in his mind and takes its abilities as his own. He is one of very few who have overcome their [[Synthetic Nymph]], the only other one being [[chrys]]. 

Connor's synthetic nymph injection is, by far, the most interesting. Though cunning and very provocative, the snake was one of the very few to be unsuccessful in possession of their host. Had this injection been anyone other than Connor, things may have been different.

He was born with brown hair and brown eyes, common for humans. After his injection and gradual transformation, his hair began to become thinner, fluffier, and lighter in color, and his eyes became larger, and deep golden, resembling the eyes of his injected nymph. Even though he would overcome this nymph, his appearance would remain changed forever.

He met [[rei]], who he would eventually marry, during the worst part of his life. While he was the sole scientist working to create power enhancing drugs for them, and being threatened, abused, and coerced into doing so. He was on the brink of madness, and if not for Rei, Mephisto may very well have succeeded in the destruction of both universes.
