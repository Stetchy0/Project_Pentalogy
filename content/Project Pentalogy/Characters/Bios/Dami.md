### Backstory & Details

<details>
<summary><b>Click Here for long text (This is quite long)</b></summary>

He was born in [[Farther Earth|farther earth]]. He was mentioned multiple times in the [[Part 0 - Prolegomenon]], but is formally introduced in [[Part III - Hope]].

Damian Ivanov was born shortly before [[Part II - Sanctuary|Sanctuary]]. When he was born, there were complications, and his mother, [[ameille]], passed away, leaving [[pherenix|his father]] to take care of him and his [[persephone|older sister]] alone. [[connor]] and [[rei]], as well as their children [[lucielle]] and eventually [[brook]], would help Pherenix take care of them, because on top of the grief he was facing, he also had a newborn child to care for. Dami's oldest memories revolve around his extended family, playing with him as he cooed, while his father and sister mourned.

When he was around 3 years old, [[mephisto]] used his abilities to lead him into an active [[Black Crusaders|black crusader]] base, to [[Connor's Machine]], which Mephisto also orchestrated. This machine, at this point in time, was a time machine. Dami would be it's only user. He was sent back in time, hundreds of years, so that he would be raised by [[faust]], Mephisto's most trusted follower. This era was close to the beginning of time, when the borders between the dimensions were softer. This time travelling, in fact, is the reason his hair has greyed at such a young age. The other side effects from time travelling aren't as noticeable like hair is, such as exceedingly fast fingernail growth, year long comatose state, and skin fragility. 

Mephisto had much more power during the time he was sent to, specifically because both worlds were in utter chaos, between wars (namely, the [[Salvation War]], which was happening around Faust and Dami at this point in time), famine, death, and destruction. This allowed for Mephisto to briefly possess Dami multiple times over the course of a few years, and corrupt him with ambition for power, and lead him on to want world domination. Faust also fed him these lies, but he wasn't happy to do so.

As the Salvation war began to end, and Faust's death became imminent, Mephisto possessed him briefly to launch him forward in time, back into the bunker, where he would blend into the crusader ranks and gain notoriety, putting him on a path towards leadership roles. He began as a scout for [[antoinette]]'s crusaders. She would be the one to rejuvenate the use of [[Anthracaine]], as he "somehow finds" Connor's research describing its creation process. This act alone would put him in line to become a general, which he does in [[Part IV - Truth]], after many of Ant's generals are killed or deserting after [[Glass Balcony|her defeat]] by [[aiko]]'s hand.

</details>
