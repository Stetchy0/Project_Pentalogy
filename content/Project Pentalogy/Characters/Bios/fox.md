Forest Gauthier, otherwise known as General Fox of the [[Black Crusaders]], contains a synthetic fox nymph. He is stealthy, calculating and intimidating, but also rational and compassionate. He was born in [[Farther Earth|farther earth]]. He was introduced in [[Part 2.2 - Goner]].

He had been friendly with [[bunny]] before his promotion, and his friendship with [[s|Seraphine]] helps him confront his grief with losing [[Margot Auclaire]].
