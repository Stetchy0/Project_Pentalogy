The pursuit of power is a trait shared by every leader of the [[Black Crusaders]], including Mephisto. It is the major flaw that would be their downfall.

X had a strong desire for knowledge of power.
Marx had the desire to continue in X's shadow, creating a militant organization, with power in numbers.
Antoinette used fear to control her own subjects and farther earth, overpowering any resistance
