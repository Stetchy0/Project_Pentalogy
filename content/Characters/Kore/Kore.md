Kore (Core-ay) is the pseudonym that Persephone takes while she is employed by [[Antoinette]]'s crusaders. She had been cast out by [[Project Hope Effect|Team Hope]] because they suspected she was the rat, even though it wasn't her. She was captured by them, and because she was broken spirited, she gave in.

Antoinette commanded her to spy on her former allies, and she initially refused, but Ant resorted to the usage of [[Connor's Formula|extremely potent Anthracyllin]] to force her to be dependent and obedient. She puts on the crusader uniform, as well as a red wig and yellow glasses in an attempt to hide her identity. Loki would recognize her anyways, and this would be Persephone's saving grace.
