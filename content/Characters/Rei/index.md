Rei is [[Connor]]'s wife and mother to their child, [[Lucielle]]. She is the main contributing factor to Connor finding hope to escape the [[Black Crusaders]]. He was beginning to push aside his humanity so he would survive among the ranks, but Rei brought him back to reality and sanity, as well as [[Leonidas/Lee|Leo]], however, she was unable to save Leo because of the chaos caused by [[Natz's Coup D'état]].

She was born in [[Farther Earth|farther earth]]. She was introduced in [[Part I - Experience|Experience]].
