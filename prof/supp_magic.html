MAGIC

Magic, in its most primitive form, is Chaos. It removes balance from what is, by drawing from the energies of existence, (in both additive and subtractive manners), and transforming something in some way. The only magicians capable of true creation would be the two celestials, Janus would be:

- Alex, who becomes Abaddon's torch.

- Dami, who becomes Mephisto's torch.

- Persephone, who is the Singularity.

- Len, who is the Echtheurgic Janus.

- X, who is the Unechtheurgic Janus.
